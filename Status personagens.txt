Alguns personagens tem status mínimos para mudar o main status de algum artefato então entre parênteses vai ter a condicional, além de que alguns personagens podem desempenhar papel de dps ou suporte:
Aino
  conjunto: Silken Moon's Serenade (4)
  ampulheta/cálice/coroa:  Energy Recharge  ou  Elemental Mastery /  Elemental Mastery / Elemental Mastery ou CRIT Rate (Only recommended if using a Favonius weapon)
  substats: Energy Recharge >Elemental Mastery >CRIT Rate (Only recommended if using a Favonius weapon )
  recarga: 180 
  talentos: burst > skill 
Albedo
  conjunto: Husk of Opulent Dreams (4) or Golden Troupe (4)
  ampulheta/cálice/coroa: Def% / Def% or Geo% (DEF% is generally preferred for team damage unless Albedo already has, or is close to 3,000 DEF in combat.)  / Crit Rate or Crit Dmg
  substats: CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG > DEF% > Energy Recharge
  recarga: 100 (não é muito necessário focar em recarga)
  talentos: skill > burst (Not used in quite a few teams.)
Alhaitham
  conjunto: Gilded Dreams (4)
  ampulheta/cálice/coroa: Elemental Mastery or  ATK% / Dendro DMG / CRIT Rate or CRIT DMG
  substats: Energy Recharge (Certain Quicken teams can run little to no Energy Recharge and simply Burst every other rotation without a dps loss) > CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG > Elemental Mastery 	(If you're running under 200 Elemental Mastery this substat has priority over Crit)  > ATK%
  recarga: 130
  talentos: skill > burst = na
Aloy
  conjunto: Blizzard Strayer (4)
  ampulheta/cálice/coroa: ATK% or Elemental Mastery (Elemental Mastery is only an option if your Aloy is played on a Reverse Melt team as it provides nothing to a Freeze Aloy.) / Cryo% / 
	CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG
  substats: CRIT Rate or CRIT DMG > ATK% > Elemental Mastery ( Only an option if your Aloy is played on a Reverse Melt team as it provides nothing to a Freeze Aloy.) > Energy Recharge
  recarga: 110
  talentos: Burst > skill
